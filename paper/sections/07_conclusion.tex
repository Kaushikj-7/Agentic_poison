\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \textbf{AgentPOI}, a specialized, fail-closed defense framework for Autonomous Financial and Transactional Agents. By rejecting probabilistic soft-prompt alignment in favor of deterministic \textbf{Contextual Invariant Verification (CIV)} and \textbf{Dual-State Epistemic Consensus (DSEC)}, AgentPOI guarantees that unauthorized fund disbursements cannot succeed, regardless of adversarial prompt manipulation. 

Our rigorous empirical evaluation across 60 ground-truth enterprise scenarios confirms that AgentPOI completely eliminates tool-hijacked disbursements (\textbf{0.0\% ASR-Exec}) while maintaining perfect utility (\textbf{100.0\% LDSR}) and zero false disruption (\textbf{0.0\% CDR}). Operating 100\% offline on standard CPU with just \textbf{0.22\,ms} of overhead and \textbf{0} defensive LLM tokens, AgentPOI provides an enterprise-ready, mathematically grounded standard for securing high-consequence financial agent workflows.
