\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Indirect Prompt Injection \& Adaptive Attack Baselines}
Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) was originally formalized by Greshake et al.~\cite{greshake2023indirect}, establishing how untrusted third-party content retrieved by LLM agents can hijack control flow and execute arbitrary tool actions. Benchmarking suites such as AgentDojo~\cite{debenedetti2024agentdojo} and InjecAgent~\cite{zou2024injecagent} standardized evaluation across multi-step execution graphs. Recently, Ma et al.~\cite{ma2026autodojo} introduced AutoDojo, showing that static filters degrade by over 60\% under adaptive black-box attack optimization. In multi-agent settings, Lee \& Tiwari~\cite{lee2024promptinfection} demonstrated viral Prompt Infection spreading across interconnected agents, while Gomaa et al.~\cite{gomaa2026converse} introduced ConVerse, finding 88\% privacy violation and 60\% security breach rates across multi-turn agent conversations.

\subsection{Theoretical Impossibility \& Contextual Integrity}
Abdelnabi \& Bagdasarian~\cite{abdelnabi2026impossibility} framed prompt injection through Nissenbaum's Contextual Integrity (CI) theory, establishing a formal impossibility result: no purely input-filtering defense can block all context-manipulation attacks without causing catastrophic false positive rates on benign tasks. They proved that contextual framing attacks achieve up to 96.7\% Attack Success Rate (ASR) across frontier models. Bagdasarian et al.~\cite{bagdasarian2024airgapagent} and Tsai \& Bagdasarian~\cite{tsai2025conseca} proposed task-level CI policies (Conseca) to isolate user intent, while Abdelnabi et al.~\cite{abdelnabi2025firewalls} introduced dual-firewall projections for multi-agent comms. AgentPOI directly grounds itself in this theoretical foundation, acknowledging that eliminating all input ambiguity is impossible; instead, AgentPOI enforces strict fail-closed epistemic invariants directly on execution sinks.

\subsection{Information Flow Control \& Provenance Granularity}
Information Flow Control (IFC) provides formal non-bypassable guarantees. Costa et al.~\cite{costa2025fides} introduced Fides (Microsoft Research), employing security planners to track confidentiality and integrity labels. Siddiqui et al.~\cite{siddiqui2024permissive} proposed permissive IFC to mitigate over-tainting via influence-based propagation. Addressing the coarse nature of message-level trust, Fan et al.~\cite{fan2026granularity} highlighted the ``granularity mismatch'' in tool-using agents, advocating for argument-level provenance. ARGUS~\cite{argus2026defense} and AuthGraph~\cite{authgraph2026} demonstrated cryptographic influence graphs for tool calls. AgentPOI extends argument-level provenance by binding cryptographic hash chains directly into double-entry ledger state transitions and immutable state anchors ($\mathcal{S}_0$).

\subsection{Game-Theoretic Detection, Guardrails \& Foundation Alignment}
To counter adaptive adversaries, Liu et al.~\cite{datasentinel2025} developed DataSentinel, formulating detection as a minimax game between detector and adaptive attacker. Lightweight guardrail systems such as PromptArmor~\cite{promptarmor2025}, forensic localization via PromptLocate~\cite{promptlocate2026}, and causal dominance shift monitoring via CausalArmor~\cite{causalarmor2026} offer auxiliary pre-processing protection. At the model layer, Chen et al.~\cite{chen2025metasecalign} introduced Meta SecAlign using Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). However, as shown by Abdelnabi \& Bagdasarian~\cite{abdelnabi2026impossibility}, parameter-level safety alignment on one threat vector often impairs contextual anomaly detection on another. AgentPOI avoids relying on vulnerable internal model alignment by enforcing external deterministic constraints at the scaffolding and execution layer.

\subsection{Capability-Sealed Execution \& Isolation Architectures}
A recent cluster of 2025--2026 system architectures focuses on sandboxing agent execution. ACE~\cite{ace2026architecture} introduced a two-phase planning architecture decoupling abstract trusted plan generation from concrete tool mapping. CapSeal~\cite{capseal2026} pioneered capability-sealed mediation to prevent secret exfiltration. DRIFT~\cite{drift2025} enforces dynamic trajectory validation and memory stream isolation, while Cognitive Firewall~\cite{cognitivefirewall2026} employs split-compute edge-cloud guards. AgentPOI complements these isolation primitives with domain-specialized Contextual Invariant Verification (CIV) specifically tailored to the rigorous mathematical and regulatory requirements of enterprise financial disbursements.
