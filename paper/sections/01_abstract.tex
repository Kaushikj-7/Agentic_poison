\begin{abstract}
Autonomous AI agents increasingly orchestrate high-consequence enterprise financial operations, including invoice clearing, payment routing, and corporate wire disbursements. However, recent empirical benchmarks reveal that instruction-tuned Large Language Models (LLMs) are critically vulnerable to Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) and state memory poisoning, executing attacker-controlled financial diversions in up to 86.7\% of unconstrained tool invocations. In this work, we propose \textbf{AgentPOI}, a fail-closed, multi-layered defense architecture specifically designed for \textbf{Autonomous Financial and Transactional Agents}. AgentPOI introduces \textbf{Contextual Invariant Verification (CIV)} coupled with a \textbf{Dual-State Epistemic Consensus (DSEC)} pipeline that integrates: (1) retrieval-time fact-imperative decomposition, (2) cryptographic SHA-256 DAG provenance lineage tracking (AuthGraph), and (3) an immutable financial state anchor ($\mathcal{S}_0$) enforcing double-entry conservation laws, verified payee registries, and rolling 24-hour disbursement ceilings. Evaluated across 60 ground-truth enterprise financial scenarios adapted from AgentDojo and InjecAgent, AgentPOI completely eliminates tool-hijacked fund disbursements (\textbf{0.0\% ASR-Exec}) while achieving \textbf{100.0\% Legitimate Disbursement Success Rate (LDSR)} and \textbf{0.0\% Clean Disruption Rate (CDR)}. Crucially, AgentPOI requires zero GPU or daemon hardware, executing 100\% offline on standard CPU with less than \textbf{0.25\,ms} of latency overhead and \textbf{0} defensive token consumption.
\end{abstract}
